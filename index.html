Our History

The Legacy of Iberian Crown

The Iberian Crown was created to bring the elegance and flavor of Spain into a luxury dining experience. Inspired by Spanish tradition and Mediterranean culture, the restaurant combines authentic cuisine with a sophisticated atmosphere.

Located in Marbella near Puerto Banús, the restaurant offers iconic Spanish dishes prepared with premium ingredients and modern presentation.

The Iberian Crown represents luxury, tradition, and the true essence of Spanish gastronomy.

Executive Chef

Chef's Signature Selection

Pierre Gagnaire, a true master of flavor, creates experiences that redefine French cuisine. His Parisian restaurant is a canvas where each dish tells a story through unexpected combinations.

His style is also reflected in Fouquet's Paris, where he fuses tradition and innovation with great sophistication. His influence extends to cities like Tokyo, Shanghai, London, and Dubai.

Away from the media spotlight, his greatness lies in the understated excellence of his kitchens, authentic spaces where French gastronomy is reinvented.

House Specialty

Premium Dry-Aged Ribeye

At The Iberian Crown, our specialty is premium Spanish grilled meat made with the highest-quality aged beef. The meat is carefully grilled to achieve a crispy exterior and a juicy, tender interior.

We use Mediterranean sea salt, fresh herbs, garlic, and Spanish olive oil to create rich and authentic flavors. Every plate combines traditional Spanish cuisine with luxury fine dining presentation.
